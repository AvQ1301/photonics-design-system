# Components

All values are px on the 1920 × 1080 canvas. `templates/photonics-template.pptx` contains each pattern.

## Numbered step

- Circle: 80 × 80, Red.
- Numeral: FS Magistral Bold Italic, 40px, White, centered.
- Title: 38px Regular, Ink, 24px right of the circle.
- Description: 24px Regular, Ink, under the title.
- Use three steps at most.

## Table

- Header: Panel fill, 24px Bold, 2px Mist rule below.
- Label column: 24px Bold. Use Brick for task IDs.
- Cell: 24px Regular, with 24px padding.
- Rules: horizontal 2px Mist only, with no side rules.

## Callout panel

- Fill: Panel.
- Top edge: 8px Red, full width.
- Eyebrow: 24px Bold caps, +1.92px tracking, Brick, inset 32px.
- Boxes inside the panel: White fill with a 2px Mist edge.

## Comparison

- Shared panel: Panel fill across the right column.
- Option cards: two 548 × 218 cards with a 32px gap.
- Card edge: 2px Mist for the reference, 2px Red for the option under test.
- Conclusion: 8px Red rule, then a 30px Bold line and a 24px line.

## Stat card

- Card: 548 × 172, 2px Mist edge, no fill.
- Value: 54px Bold, Brick.
- Label: 24px Regular, Ink.
- Give every value its unit in the same text.

## Note

Use 24px Regular in Slate at y 889, for scope, status, or a starting point.

## Link

Use Brick with an underline.
