# Citation

Cite as `Author et al. (year)`.

```text
Macenski et al. (2020)
```

## Rules

- Do not add a table or figure number.
- Do not write a label such as `Source:` before the citation.
- Style it in Brick and underline it when it links to the work.
- Put the full reference and link in the speaker notes, under `Sources:`.

## Examples

| Use | Avoid |
|---|---|
| Macenski et al. (2020) | Source: Macenski et al. (2020) |
| Macenski et al. (2020) | Macenski et al. (2020), Table II |
| Chen et al. (2019) | Figure 1, Chen et al., 2019 |
