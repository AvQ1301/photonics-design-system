# Layout

All values are px on the 1920 × 1080 canvas.

## Slide frame

| Element | Position | Size | Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| Title | x 48, y 25 | 84 tall | 60px Bold, Ink |
| Title rule | x 48, y 117 | 288 × 12 | Red |
| Content | starts at y 181 | | |
| Left column | x 48 to 650 | | Text blocks |
| Right column | x 728 to 1856 | 1128 wide | Tables, figures, panels |
| Note | x 48, y 889 | 604 wide | 24px Regular, Slate |
| Footer band | x 0, y 1032 | 1920 × 48 | Stone |
| Page tab | x 1656, y 1032 | 264 × 48 | Hot, rounded |
| Page number | right edge at x 1899 | | 30px Bold, White |

## Slide types

| Type | Structure |
|---|---|
| Cover | Centered title at y 281, presenter at y 391, three 24px lines at y 491. No title rule. |
| Section | Centered 64px title at y 274, three 24px lines at y 374. No title rule. |
| Outline | Three numbered steps on the left, a full-height image area from x 960, a citation at y 978. |
| Content | Left text blocks with a table, figure, or panel on the right. |

## Text blocks

A left-column block is a 38px Bold heading, then 30px body text with a 45px line height.

- Heading to body: 10px gap.
- Block to block: 26px gap.
- Use three blocks of at most two body lines.

## Spacing

Keep nothing below y 1032 except the footer. Keep text inside x 48 to 1856, except for centered cover and section text.
